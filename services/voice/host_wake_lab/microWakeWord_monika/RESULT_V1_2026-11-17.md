(«Моник», «Моня», «Монику», «монитор»), conversational mentions
(«с Моникой») and at least some unrelated phrases. This candidate
is **REJECTED** for production wake and is NOT an installed model.
Synthetic negative minutes are not a representative ambient home
false-awakening rate. p50/p95 actual human latency is NOT_MEASURED.

This first iteration demonstrates actual training and genuine integer
quantized streaming export, not wake reliability. The next v2 experimental
recipe increases hard-negative source coverage, removes ambiguous
first-token «Моника» from the negative class, increases negative
training emphasis, and keeps independently synthesized test speech.
Do not mix v1 and v2 weights or claim previous speaker evaluation of
v1 proves anything for v2.
